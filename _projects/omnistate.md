description: "A reinforcement learning policy trained only in simulation, on 20,480 parallel Isaac Lab robots, inserts pegs on a real Franka at the 0.02 mm ManipulationNet tolerance, tighter than any it saw in training (per-shape hardware evaluation in progress). It commands full 6-D pose and impedance and senses contact only through the arm's joint torques. During training, simulated instances of the robot with different hidden board and grasp poses share one action, so the policy learns actions that work for every pose it cannot yet rule out."
